About the Lab

Stellar SAR Lab is an independent research initiative focused on empirical questions in AI safety and alignment. We investigate how learned objectives and evaluation systems behave under optimization, and how their reliability can be assessed.

Research directions

What We Study

Evaluator Reliability

Investigating the reliability of learned evaluators as language models face increasing selection and optimization pressure.

Reward Modeling

Studying reward models, preference learning, and the relationship between proxy scores and intended objectives.

AI Alignment & Safety

Empirical investigation of alignment-related behavior, reward hacking, and evaluation robustness.

Model Evaluation

Developing controlled evaluation methods and reproducible benchmarks for language-model behavior.
